# Project Apollo — Operations Guide (Team Beta)

Sample document for evaluating the Document Assistant. Upload it to **Workspace B** only.
All names and facts are fictional.

## Stack

Project Apollo uses MongoDB 8 as its primary database. Backups are kept for 30 days.
The service runs on Node.js and is deployed to the Mumbai region.

## Support policy

Customer refunds can be requested within 45 days of purchase. Enterprise customers get a
dedicated support channel with a 4-hour response time.

## Maintenance

Planned maintenance happens on the first Sunday of every month, from 02:00 to 04:00 IST.
Customers are notified at least 72 hours in advance.
